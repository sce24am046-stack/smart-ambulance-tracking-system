Operational Lifecycle

How ResQ Pulse Coordinates Emergencies

STEP 01

Emergency Intake

Citizen or operator registers caller info, GPS/address, and category. System applies transparent rule-based priority triage (Cardiac/Accident -> High).

STEP 02

Smart Dispatch Assist

Haversine engine evaluates all 'Available' fleet units, computes distance & estimated ETA, and highlights the optimal ALS/BLS candidate for dispatcher sign-off.

STEP 03

En Route Telemetry

Assigned unit transitions to 'Busy'. Real-time coordinates update on command center radar. Requesters track status via privacy-safe public reference.

STEP 04

Hospital Handover

Ambulance completes transport at nearest specialized medical facility. Vehicle status automatically returns to 'Available' for the next mission.
